# Finding Rating and Remediation

Use the organization's approved rating methodology. Common factors include:
- Impact to financial reporting, customers, operations, security, privacy, compliance, and reputation.
- Likelihood and exposure.
- Population size and duration.
- Recurrence and systemic root cause.
- Compensating controls and their precision.
- Management response and residual risk.

A remediation plan should specify root cause, action, accountable owner, milestones, target date, dependencies, success criteria, closure evidence, and independent validation. Do not close an issue solely because management marked it complete.
