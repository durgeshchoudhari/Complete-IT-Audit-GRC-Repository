# Sample IT Audit Report — Fictional Case

**Engagement:** Fictional Finance Application ITGC Review  
**Period:** Q3 2026  
**Status:** Training example only

## 1. Objective
Assess whether selected access management, change management, and backup monitoring controls are designed and operating to address defined risks.

## 2. Scope
Fictional finance application, related IAM records, production change tickets, and backup job records. Sample datasets in this repository are deliberately small and synthetic; they are not a representative audit population.

## 3. Approach
Reviewed sample datasets, performed scripted screening, and identified records requiring follow-up. No real system evidence was inspected.

## 4. Preliminary observations
- Access data includes a terminated employee with an enabled account and records without approval/review indicators.
- Change data includes records without recorded approval, testing, or emergency post-implementation review.
- Backup data includes a failed job without recorded alert, ticket, or successful rerun.

## 5. Limitations
These are data-quality and control-testing exercises only. Population completeness, underlying policy criteria, actual timestamps, source reliability, and management explanations have not been validated. Therefore, no formal audit opinion or confirmed finding is issued.

## 6. Required follow-up
Obtain source reports and parameters, policy/SLA, ticket evidence, logs, owner explanations, and remediation proof. Complete workpapers and apply the organization's approved issue-rating methodology before reporting.
