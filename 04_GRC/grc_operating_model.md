# GRC Operating Model

## Governance
Board/committee oversight, strategy, risk appetite, policies, roles, accountability, escalation, and performance reporting.

## Risk management
Identify, assess, respond to, monitor, and report risks. Distinguish inherent risk from residual risk after considering controls. Define likelihood and impact scales and document assumptions.

## Compliance management
Maintain obligations inventory, map obligations to policies and controls, assign owners, monitor changes, evidence compliance, and manage exceptions.

## Typical GRC artifacts
Risk register, RCSA, control library, policy register, compliance obligations register, issue register, KRI dashboard, exception register, committee reporting, and action tracker.
