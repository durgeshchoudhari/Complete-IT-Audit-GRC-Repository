# Policy, Compliance, and Issue Management

A policy lifecycle typically includes drafting, ownership, approval, publication, awareness, periodic review, exception handling, and retirement/version control.

Maintain a compliance obligations register with source, obligation, applicability, owner, mapped controls, evidence, review frequency, and change-monitoring process.

Issue management should capture source, risk, root cause, rating, accountable owner, action, target date, dependencies, milestones, closure evidence, validation, and escalation. Closure should be evidence-based, not status-only.
