# Risk Assessment, RCSA, and KRIs

## Risk statement
Use a clear cause-event-impact structure: "Because of [cause], [event] may occur, resulting in [impact]."

## Assessment
Assess inherent likelihood and impact before controls. Assess control design and operation. Determine residual risk using the organization's approved methodology. Do not use false precision.

## RCSA
A Risk and Control Self-Assessment is a structured process where business/control owners assess risks and controls, with challenge and oversight from risk functions.

## KRI examples
- Percentage of terminated users disabled within SLA.
- Number of overdue critical vulnerabilities.
- Percentage of emergency changes receiving required retrospective review.
- Failed backup jobs unresolved beyond threshold.
- High-risk vendors with overdue reassessment.

Define numerator, denominator, source, owner, threshold, frequency, and escalation for each KRI.
