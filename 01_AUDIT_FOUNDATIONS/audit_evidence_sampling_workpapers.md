# Evidence, Sampling, and Workpapers

## Evidence quality
Assess relevance to the audit objective, reliability of source, sufficiency, period coverage, authenticity, and traceability. System-generated reports should retain report parameters, timestamps, source, filters, and record counts. Consider whether data was modified or manually manipulated.

## Population validation checklist
- Is the source system authoritative?
- Does the period match the audit scope?
- Are all relevant entities and systems included?
- Are filters and exclusions understood?
- Can record count be reconciled to a trusted source?
- Are duplicates, missing records, and null values understood?
- Is the report owner and extraction method documented?

## Workpaper minimum fields
Engagement, objective, risk/control reference, period, population source/count, sample method/size, test steps, evidence reference, result, exception, reviewer, date, and conclusion.

## Common sample methods
Random, systematic, judgmental, targeted/high-risk, and full-population analytics. Document why the method is appropriate. Targeted selections do not necessarily support statistical projection.
