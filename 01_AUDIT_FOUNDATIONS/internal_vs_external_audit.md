# Internal Audit vs External Audit

| Dimension | Internal audit | External audit |
|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Independent assurance/advisory to the board and management on governance, risk management, and controls | Independent opinion on financial statements under applicable auditing standards |
| Scope | Risk-based and can cover operations, technology, compliance, governance, and strategic risks | Financial statements and related audit requirements; may consider relevant controls to design procedures |
| Reporting | Typically to the board/audit committee, with management communication | Shareholders or other intended users under the applicable reporting framework |
| Independence | Organizational independence and objectivity are essential | Independence requirements apply to the audit firm and engagement team |
| Output | Internal audit reports, observations, ratings, recommendations | Auditor's report and communications required by standards/law |
| Control testing | May directly assess design and operating effectiveness across broad areas | Tests controls when relevant to audit strategy or required; does not automatically opine on all controls |

Internal and external auditors may coordinate where permitted, but responsibilities, objectives, standards, and conclusions remain distinct. Management owns the controls; auditors do not operate them.
