# IT Operations, Backup, and Resilience Audit

Review batch jobs, backups, monitoring, incident management, problem management, capacity, availability, disaster recovery, and business continuity interfaces.

For backup controls, assess job schedules, failure alerts, incident tickets, reruns, retention, encryption, access restrictions, offsite/immutable copies where relevant, and restore-test outcomes.

For resilience, understand business impact analysis, RTO, RPO, dependency mapping, DR strategy, exercise scope, test results, issues, and remediation. A successful backup log does not prove recoverability; inspect restore evidence.
