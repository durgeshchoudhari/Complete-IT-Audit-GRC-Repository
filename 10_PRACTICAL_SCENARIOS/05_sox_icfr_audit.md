# Scenario 5 — SOX / ICFR

**Risk:** A material financial reporting process may be exposed to unauthorized access, inaccurate processing, or incomplete reporting.

**Control objective:** Relevant financial reporting risks are addressed by suitably designed and operating controls.

**Evidence:** Process narratives, walkthroughs, RCM, account mapping, application inventory, report logic, ITGC evidence, test sheets, issue evaluations.

**Scenario questions**
1. How do you identify in-scope applications?
2. What makes a control a key control?
3. How do you connect ITGCs to financial reporting?
4. What is the difference between a control deficiency and a material weakness?
5. What if a control owner cannot provide evidence?
6. How do you evaluate compensating controls?
