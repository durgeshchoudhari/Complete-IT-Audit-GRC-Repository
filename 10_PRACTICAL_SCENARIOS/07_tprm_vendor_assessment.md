# Scenario 7 — Third-Party Risk Management

**Risk:** A vendor may expose sensitive data, critical services, or systems to security, continuity, compliance, or concentration risk.

**Control objective:** Vendors are risk-tiered, assessed, contractually governed, monitored, and securely offboarded.

**Evidence:** Vendor inventory, intake form, risk tiering, questionnaire, SOC report, ISO scope, penetration-test summary, contract clauses, incident history, BCP/DR, reassessment, exit checklist.

**Scenario questions**
1. How do you determine inherent vendor risk?
2. What if a critical vendor refuses an audit right?
3. How do you assess a SOC report?
4. What is fourth-party risk?
5. How do you track remediation?
6. What happens when a vendor relationship ends?
