# Scenario 9 — Internal and External Audit Coordination

**Situation:** Internal audit and external financial statement auditors request overlapping evidence.

**Questions**
1. How do you coordinate requests without compromising independence?
2. Can external auditors rely on internal audit work?
3. What factors affect the external auditor's evaluation of internal audit objectivity, competence, and systematic approach?
4. Who owns remediation?
5. How do you handle conflicting conclusions?

**Key point:** Coordination can reduce duplication, but each auditor retains responsibility for their own objectives, standards, evidence evaluation, and conclusions. Management owns the controls and remediation.
