# Scenario 3 — IT Operations, Backup, and DR

**Risk:** Failed processing or unavailable data may cause financial, operational, or customer impact.

**Control objective:** Jobs and backups are monitored, failures are investigated and resolved, and recovery capability is periodically validated.

**Evidence:** Job schedules, logs, alerts, tickets, rerun results, backup configuration, retention settings, restore tests, BIA, RTO/RPO, DR exercise report.

**Scenario questions**
1. Does a successful backup log prove recoverability?
2. How would you test a failed job?
3. What is the difference between RTO and RPO?
4. What if the DR test was completed but findings remain open?
5. How do you validate backup job population completeness?
