# Scenario 8 — Framework-Based GRC Assessment

**Risk:** Governance and control activities may not address enterprise technology and cybersecurity risks consistently.

**Control objective:** Relevant framework outcomes and governance objectives are mapped to accountable controls and monitored evidence.

**Evidence:** Policies, risk register, control library, NIST CSF profile, COBIT objective mapping, COSO component mapping, KRIs, committee minutes, issue register.

**Scenario questions**
1. How would you use NIST CSF to assess cybersecurity posture?
2. What is the difference between NIST CSF and NIST SP 800-53?
3. How do COSO components support internal control?
4. How would COBIT help assess IT governance?
5. How do you avoid duplicate controls in a cross-framework mapping?
