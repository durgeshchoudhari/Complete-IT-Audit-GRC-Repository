# Scenario 2 — Change Management / Emergency Changes

**Risk:** Unauthorized, untested, or improperly deployed changes may disrupt systems or alter financial processing.

**Control objective:** Changes are assessed, approved, tested, and deployed through controlled processes; emergency changes follow an approved expedited process and required retrospective review.

**Evidence:** Change population, tickets, impact assessment, approvals, test/UAT results, code/release logs, deployment logs, emergency review records.

**Scenario questions**
1. How do you identify emergency changes?
2. How do you test approval timing?
3. What if emergency approval is retrospective?
4. How do you check developer/deployer segregation?
5. What if the sample has no UAT evidence but a technical test exists?
6. How do you identify direct production changes?
7. What trends would you report to management?
