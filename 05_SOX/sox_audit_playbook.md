# SOX / ICFR Audit Playbook

## Scoping
Understand financial statement accounts, disclosures, significant processes, locations, applications, reports, interfaces, and service organizations relevant to ICFR. Scope is determined using applicable methodology and materiality/risk considerations.

## Process walkthrough
Trace significant transaction types from initiation through recording and reporting. Identify manual controls, automated controls, IT dependencies, key reports, interfaces, and relevant ITGCs.

## Key control documentation
Document risk, assertion, control objective, owner, frequency, precision, evidence, systems, report dependencies, and control performer/reviewer.

## Testing
Evaluate design and operating effectiveness. Validate population completeness and accuracy. Select samples based on methodology. Inspect evidence and record deviations.

## Deficiency evaluation
Evaluate the nature, likelihood, and potential magnitude of misstatement, individually and in aggregate, using applicable requirements. Consider compensating controls only if they operate at sufficient precision and are evidenced. Classification terms and thresholds depend on applicable rules and methodology.

## Common IT dependencies
Access to programs/data, program changes, computer operations, interfaces, automated calculations, system-generated reports, and end-user computing.

## Interview point
SOX testing is not simply checking whether a document exists. The auditor must understand the financial reporting risk, control precision, evidence, population, period, and potential misstatement.
