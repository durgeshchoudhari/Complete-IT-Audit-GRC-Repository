# COBIT — Governance and Management of Enterprise I&T

COBIT provides a framework for governance and management of enterprise information and technology. It distinguishes governance from management and organizes objectives across domains:
- **EDM:** Evaluate, Direct and Monitor.
- **APO:** Align, Plan and Organize.
- **BAI:** Build, Acquire and Implement.
- **DSS:** Deliver, Service and Support.
- **MEA:** Monitor, Evaluate and Assess.

Use COBIT to discuss governance objectives, accountability, alignment, performance, risk, and control practices. It is not the same as a detailed audit program; tailor objectives and practices to enterprise context and consult current ISACA materials.
