# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 — Overview

NIST CSF 2.0 organizes cybersecurity risk outcomes into six Functions:
- **Govern:** establish and monitor cybersecurity risk strategy, expectations, policy, roles, and oversight.
- **Identify:** understand assets, suppliers, systems, data, and cybersecurity risks.
- **Protect:** implement safeguards such as identity management, awareness, data security, and platform protection.
- **Detect:** identify and analyze potential cybersecurity events.
- **Respond:** manage, communicate, analyze, mitigate, and improve during incidents.
- **Recover:** restore capabilities and improve recovery planning.

Use CSF outcomes to structure a profile, understand current/target posture, identify gaps, and prioritize actions. CSF is not a prescriptive checklist or a certification. For detailed control requirements, use an appropriate control catalog (for example, NIST SP 800-53) when required by scope and context. Always consult the current official NIST publication.
