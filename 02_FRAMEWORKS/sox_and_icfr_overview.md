# SOX and Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR)

SOX is United States legislation. Section 404 requirements relate to management's assessment of internal control over financial reporting and, for applicable issuers, auditor attestation. Applicability and requirements depend on entity status, rules, and current law.

IT controls can affect ICFR when systems, reports, interfaces, and data support financial reporting. Common ITGC domains include access, change management, and IT operations. IT application controls include automated validations, calculations, interfaces, and workflow approvals.

A control deficiency assessment considers likelihood and magnitude of potential misstatement, individually and in combination, under the applicable framework and methodology. Do not assume every exception is a material weakness. Consult qualified accounting/legal guidance and current SEC/PCAOB requirements where applicable.
