# COSO Internal Control — Integrated Framework

COSO's Internal Control—Integrated Framework is commonly described through five components:
1. Control Environment
2. Risk Assessment
3. Control Activities
4. Information and Communication
5. Monitoring Activities

The framework supports designing, implementing, and assessing internal control. It is broader than IT controls and can be applied to financial reporting, operations, and compliance objectives. Map controls to the relevant objective and component; do not assume a COSO mapping alone proves control effectiveness.
