# SOC Reports: SOC 1 and SOC 2

## SOC 1
Focuses on controls at a service organization relevant to user entities' internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). Often relevant when outsourced services affect financial reporting processes.

## SOC 2
Reports on controls relevant to one or more Trust Services Criteria categories: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy. The categories included depend on the engagement scope.

## Type I vs Type II
- **Type I:** evaluates description and suitability of design as of a specified date.
- **Type II:** evaluates description, suitability of design, and operating effectiveness over a specified period.

## Review a report
Check service organization, report period, system description, auditor's opinion, scope, criteria, control tests and results, exceptions, management response, subservice organization method (carve-out or inclusive), and CUECs.

A clean SOC report does not eliminate user-entity responsibilities. Map CUECs to the user's own controls and verify they are implemented. A SOC report is an independent attestation report, not a general-purpose certification.
