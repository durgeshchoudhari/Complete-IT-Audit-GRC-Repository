# IT Audit & GRC Scenario-Based Interview Question Bank

Use the answer pattern: **Context → Risk → Control objective → Evidence → Test procedure → Exception handling → Conclusion**.

## Audit lifecycle
1. Walk me through an audit from planning to closure.
2. How do you prepare an audit plan?
3. How do you identify key risks and controls?
4. Explain a walkthrough and how it differs from OE testing.
5. How do you validate a population?
6. How do you decide sample size?
7. What makes audit evidence reliable?
8. How do you handle scope limitations?
9. How do you communicate a disputed finding?
10. How do you follow up overdue remediation?

## ITGC / IAM
11. How do you test joiner-mover-leaver controls?
12. How do you verify timely leaver disablement?
13. How do you test least privilege?
14. How do you review privileged access?
15. What is SoD and how would you identify conflicts?
16. What if a manager approves access without understanding the role?
17. What if access review is completed but no evidence of challenge exists?

## Change management

19. How do you test emergency changes?
20. What if emergency approval is retrospective?
21. How do you test segregation between developer and deployer?
22. How do you identify direct production changes?
23. What evidence proves deployment matches the approved version?
