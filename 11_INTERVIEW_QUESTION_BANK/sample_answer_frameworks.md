# Sample Answer Frameworks

## "How do you ensure a user genuinely needs access?"
"I would validate the business justification against the user's job responsibilities and approved role catalogue. I would inspect manager and application/data owner approvals, check whether the requested role is least-privilege, assess SoD conflicts, and verify that provisioning matches the approved request. For privileged access, I would expect stronger approval, time-bound access where appropriate, logging, and periodic review."

## "How do you ensure emergency changes are approved?"
"I would first understand the organization's emergency change policy. Then I would obtain the full emergency change population and inspect justification, required authorization, risk assessment, testing, deployment evidence, and retrospective review within the policy timeline. I would investigate repeated emergency usage and any deviations."

## "What if evidence is missing?"
"I would clarify whether the evidence exists in another system or format, request alternative corroborating evidence, and assess whether the population and control operation can still be substantiated. I would document the limitation, expand procedures if appropriate, and evaluate the impact under the audit methodology. I would not assume the control operated merely because the owner says it did."

## "How do you rate a finding?"
"I would follow the approved rating methodology and consider likelihood, impact, scope, duration, recurrence, affected population, compensating controls, and root cause. For SOX/ICFR, I would separately apply the relevant deficiency evaluation requirements rather than using a generic IT risk rating."

## "What have you automated?"
"For this learning repository, I wrote Python checks that flag selected patterns in fictional CSV data, such as enabled accounts for terminated users and failed backup jobs without recorded follow-up. I treat the output as an investigation queue. Before reaching a conclusion, I validate source completeness, accuracy, criteria, and supporting evidence."
