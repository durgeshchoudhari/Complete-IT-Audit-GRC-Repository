# Learning Roadmap: Beginner to Practical IT Audit & GRC

## Phase 1 — Foundations
- Understand assurance, consulting/advisory, independence, objectivity, and professional skepticism.
- Learn audit universe, risk-based planning, scope, criteria, materiality, evidence, sampling, and workpapers.
- Deliverable: audit charter, planning memo, audit program, and evidence request list.

## Phase 2 — Framework literacy
- Understand the purpose and structure of NIST CSF 2.0, COSO Internal Control—Integrated Framework, and COBIT.
- Understand SOC 1 vs SOC 2 and Type I vs Type II.
- Understand SOX/ICFR at a high level.
- Deliverable: framework crosswalk and control mapping.

## Phase 3 — ITGC and ITAC
- ITGC: logical access, change management, IT operations, SDLC/program development.
- ITAC: input, processing, output, interface, automated calculations, and workflow controls.
- Deliverable: RCM and test-of-controls workpapers.

## Phase 4 — GRC and TPRM
- Risk register, RCSA, risk appetite, KRIs, policy lifecycle, issue management, third-party risk.
- Deliverable: vendor risk assessment and remediation tracker.

## Phase 5 — Audit execution
- Walkthroughs, population validation, sampling, inspection, reperformance, inquiry, observation, analytics.
- Internal audit and external audit coordination.
- Deliverable: completed scenario workpapers and draft report.

## Phase 6 — Automation and interview readiness
- Run Python checks, interpret exceptions, document limitations.
- Practice explaining risks, controls, testing, and findings without overstating experience.
- Deliverable: GitHub portfolio and interview story bank.

## Suggested 8-week schedule

| Week | Focus | Practical output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit foundations | Audit plan and scope |
| 2 | NIST, COSO, COBIT | Framework crosswalk |
| 3 | IAM and change ITGC | Two RCM rows and test sheets |
| 4 | IT operations and ITAC | Backup and transaction testing |
| 5 | SOX and SOC | ICFR and SOC report review notes |
| 6 | TPRM and GRC | Vendor assessment and risk register |
| 7 | Scenarios and reporting | Findings and audit report |
| 8 | Python and interviews | Run scripts and mock interview |

You can study faster or slower; do not treat the schedule as a certification syllabus.
