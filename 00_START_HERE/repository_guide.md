# How to Navigate This Repository

- `01_AUDIT_FOUNDATIONS`: the end-to-end audit method.
- `02_FRAMEWORKS`: what major frameworks and assurance reports are for; avoid treating them as interchangeable.
- `03_CONTROL_DOMAINS`: control objectives and typical audit procedures.
- `04_GRC`: enterprise risk and compliance operating model.
- `05_SOX`: internal control over financial reporting.
- `06_SOC`: service organization reporting and user-entity responsibilities.
- `07_TPRM`: third-party risk management lifecycle.
- `08_AUDIT_TEMPLATES`: reusable blank workpapers.
- `09_SAMPLE_DATA`: fictional evidence-like records for exercises.
- `10_PRACTICAL_SCENARIOS`: guided audit case studies.
- `11_INTERVIEW_QUESTION_BANK`: practice questions and answer structures.
- `12_REPORTING`: communicate findings and track remediation.
- `13_AUTOMATION` and `src`: beginner Python checks.

Recommended order: roadmap → audit lifecycle → control domains → templates → datasets → scenarios → report → interview bank.
